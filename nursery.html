Before you decorate

A five-step nursery checklist

Step 1

Pick a palette

Choose Meadow, Clay or Skylight — every product on this page is built to match across all three.

Step 2

Plan the crib zone

Keep the crib away from windows and cords, with the night light within reach but not overhead.

Step 3

Layer soft textures

Add the crib set, a floor rug and one framed print — three textures is usually enough.

Step 4

Lighting last

Add a dimmable night light for 2am feeds — it's the single most-used piece in the room.

Step 5

Leave growing room

Our crib converts to a toddler bed — no need to re-buy furniture at 18 months.
