How we started

A softer supply chain, on purpose.

We spent our first two years just on fabric — visiting organic cotton farms, testing dye houses, and rejecting anything that didn't feel right against a newborn's skin. That obsession never left.

Today every Stackly piece is GOTS-certified from farm to finished seam, sewn in facilities we've personally visited and audited every year since 2018.

What guides us

Three things we won't compromise on

Materials first

If a fabric or dye can't pass our skin-safety review, it doesn't ship — no exceptions, ever.

Fair, visited factories

Every partner factory is visited in person at least once a year by our own team.

Designed to circulate

Buy-back, resale and repair are part of the product, not an afterthought.
