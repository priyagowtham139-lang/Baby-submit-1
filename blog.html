Quick tips

Parenting wisdom in a nutshell

Sleep begets sleep

An overtired baby fights sleep harder. Watch for early drowsy cues — yawning, eye rubbing — and start the routine 15 minutes earlier than you think.

Layers over blankets

A fleece suit under a sleep sack is safer and warmer than a loose blanket. Think one layer more than what you'd wear yourself.

One new thing at a time

Introduce a new food, sleep change, or routine shift one at a time. Wait three days to see how your baby responds before adding another.

White noise works

Keep it below 50 dB and place the machine across the room. A steady hum mimics the womb and blocks household noise spikes.

Skin-to-skin calms

Even five minutes of bare chest contact lowers cortisol for both parent and baby. Try it before bedtime when fussiness peaks.
